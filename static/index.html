Upload photographs of alcohol beverage labels. Each one is checked against the health warning statement required by 27 CFR Part 16 and, when you supply an application record, against the brand, class/type, alcohol content and net contents on that record.

Uploaded images and results are automatically deleted every hour. This prototype runs on the developer's personal infrastructure.
